# Media Licensing, Provenance & Legal Clearance Protocol

`story-studio` operates under strict legal compliance rules to ensure every generated video is 100% safe for commercial YouTube distribution, monetization, and educational broadcast.

---

## 🏛️ Permitted Media Sources

### 1. NASA Public Domain Scientific Media
- **Source**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS).
- **Legal Status**: Public Domain under U.S. Federal Copyright Law (17 U.S.C. § 105).
- **Usage**: Free for commercial, non-commercial, and educational reproduction.
- **Attribution Policy**: While not legally required for public domain, `story-studio` logs polite professional credit in `asset-manifest.json` (*"Footage courtesy of NASA/SVS"*).

### 2. Procedural & Bespoke Vector Code
- **Source**: Remotion React components, procedural SVG diagrams, and Canvas shaders generated by the studio.
- **Legal Status**: 100% original, proprietary, or MIT licensed.
- **Usage**: Full unrestricted commercial rights with zero royalty obligations.

### 3. Neural Speech Narration (Edge-TTS)
- **Source**: Microsoft Cognitive Services Speech Synthesis.
- **Legal Status**: Permitted for video content voiceover and automated narration.

### 4. Atmospheric Music & Ambient Drones
- **Source**: Synthesized procedural cosmic tones and royalty-free creative commons zero (CC0) soundscapes.
- **Target**: Zero Content-ID claim triggers.

---

## 🚫 Strictly Prohibited Media Sources
- **Copyrighted YouTube Rips**: Never scrape or download unauthorized footage from other YouTube creators.
- **Watermarked Stock Footage**: Never use unpurchased preview footage containing visual watermarks.
- **Trademarked Logos**: Banned unless explicitly citing a documented scientific institution in an editorial context.
