For eight minutes and twenty seconds, the sky remains radiant, children laugh in the park, and Earth continues its tranquil orbit. But across the void, a silent countdown has already begun.

The Sun is not just our cosmic furnace; its colossal mass bends the fabric of space-time into a deep gravitational bowl. For five hundred seconds, Earth continues circling a ghost star. Until the final photon strikes your retina.

At eight minutes and twenty seconds, total blackout hits. The Moon instantly disappears from the night sky, robbed of its reflected mirror. But the dark is merely the first blow. Without the Sun's gravitational anchor, Earth stops curving. It slingshots in a straight tangential line into deep space at thirty kilometers every single second.

## [EXPLANATION] (50s)
Within twenty-four hours, global panic collides with physics. Photosynthesis grinds to a dead stop. Within seven days, global surface temperatures plunge below freezing. Within one year, the thermometer reads minus seventy-three degrees Celsius. The surface oceans freeze solid under kilometers of impenetrable ice, sealing off the world in an icy crypt.

Clustering around hydrothermal vents, volcanic extremophiles thrive in volcanic darkness, completely indifferent to the death of the star above.
